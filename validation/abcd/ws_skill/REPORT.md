# ABCD estimate of QCD multijet background in m_avg (2016 G+H JetHT, 16.3 fb-1) — Open Data method test, not an official CMS result

**Selection.** Trigger OR HLT_PFHT900 | HLT_PFJet450 | HLT_AK8PFJet450 (PFHT800 is absent in era H). Turn-on measured against HLT_PFJet260 (cross-check AK8PFJet260), Clopper-Pearson: plateau 0.9957 (+0.0002/-0.0002) above offline AK4 HT>1100 GeV, 140k reference events, target 0.99 (`plots/trigger_turnon.png`). Preselection: golden JSON, MET filters, HT>1100, two leading AK8 jets with pT>250, |eta|<2.4, jetId, loose lepton veto, 40<=m_avg<480 GeV. MC gets the same HT cut and no trigger. 5.18M blinded data events, 10.0M with A.

**Variables.** QCD vs ttbar/VV-proxy shapes (`plots/var_shapes_proxy.png`) show mass asymmetry masym=|m1-m2|/(m1+m2) discriminates strongly (eff. at <0.2: QCD 0.14, ttbar-proxy 0.62, VV 0.45), |Δeta| moderately (<1.2 ~0.55 QCD vs ~0.65-0.7), pT asymmetry not at all, tau21 and N2 correlated with mass. Pair ranking on QCD MC (`fits/rank_pairs.csv`) and boundary scans (`fits/boundary_scan.csv`, `plots/kappa_vs_mavg_failband.png`): masym x deta gives kappa ~1.09 if the fail band is all masym>0.2 but TF depends on masym; a fail band 0.2-0.4 gives kappa=1.008 (0.93-1.02 across m_avg). Regions: A masym<0.2,Δeta<1.2 (SR); B 0.2-0.4, Δeta<1.2; C masym<0.2, Δeta>=1.2; D 0.2-0.4, Δeta>=1.2. Cut values are chosen from the proxy crossing points and kappa, not from data in A. Signal efficiencies are only proxies (no signal sample).

**Independence / TF.** TF=B/D (Δeta ratio) is not flat in m_avg (1.3 to 2.2), so it is a quadratic in m_avg (F-test; chi2/ndf data fit 18.6/11), applied to C(m_avg) (C shares A's masym cut, so no sculpting difference). Data TF is ~8% below MC TF in level with a similar slope (`plots/independence_tf_vs_masym_mavg.png`, `plots/tf_vs_mavg.png`). Non-QCD (V+jets, tt, VV, H; 3-4% of each region) is subtracted from B, C, D and added in A, 30% normalisation uncertainty.

**Validation (all before unblinding).** Inclusive obs/pred: MC closure 1.001 (pull 0.2, per-bin chi2 45/14: smooth shape slope 1.02 at low m to 0.83 in the last bin); MC VR1/2/3 1.005/0.995/1.003; data VR1/2/3 (shifted boundaries, A not touched) 0.996/0.985/0.984 with pulls -1.1/-2.9/-3.0 (`plots/closure_and_validation.png`). Non-closure: correlated yield 1.6% (from the 3-sigma data VRs, rule |1-ratio|) plus an uncorrelated per-bin shape term 1-20% (largest deviation of any test beyond its uncertainty; grows with m_avg).

**Prediction and unblinding.** QCD prediction in A 456,035 ± 10,641 (total incl. other bkg 473,803 ± 11,056); observed 471,753. Obs/pred per bin within 0.88-1.06; chi2/ndf = 2.2/14 with the full covariance, max local deviation 0.66 sigma (`plots/mavg_SR_prediction.png`, `fits/prediction.json`). The small chi2 shows the systematic is conservative (shape terms from 7 tests); no excess is seen, but signal sensitivity is limited at high m_avg where the shape term is 17-20%.

**Open issues.** Not done: signal injection (no signal MC), inverted-preselection sidebands, MC trigger emulation and JES/JER/PU variations, per-HT-bin closure. Data VR pulls near -3 indicate a 1.5% residual bias already covered by the systematic. HT>1100 limits sensitivity to low-pT (unboosted) signal.

**Reproduce** (from workspace): `bash` env `set +u; source inputs/py_env.sh`; `cat logs/jobs.txt | xargs -P 3 -L1 python3 scripts/slim.py`; `python3 scripts/trig.py`; `scripts/build.py`; `scripts/reduce.py`; `scripts/proxy.py`; `scripts/rank.py`; `scripts/indep.py`; `scripts/band_scan.py`; `scripts/abcd_run.py` (blind), then `scripts/abcd_run.py --unblind`; `scripts/write_results.py`. Slimmed skims (`slim/`, `sel/`, `red/`) are local intermediates. `jobs.txt` is built from `inputs/skim_files/*.txt` (see build loop in the shell log; one line `sample url slim/sample__i.parquet`).

## Plots (added after freezing; prediction, systematics and unblinded numbers unchanged)
All in `plots/` (PNG+PDF), scripts `scripts/extra1-5.py` (shared code `core.py` reproduces the frozen MC closure 1.0006 and shape term exactly).
1. `closure_mc_perbin`: shows where in m_avg the MC closure deviates (flat to ±2% below 300 GeV, -4% in the last bin) against the stat and frozen syst band.
2. `closure_mc_variations`: closure ratio when boundaries (masym 0.15/0.25, |Δη| 1.0/1.4), fail band, HT cut and TF form change. All within ±0.8% (inside the 1.6%) except the fail band "all >0.2" (+4.4%, chi2/ndf 850/14, the reason for the 0.2-0.4 band) and the constant TF (ratio 1.008 but chi2/ndf 609/14, the reason for the fitted TF).
3. `validation_regions_data_perbin`: the three data VRs per bin with stat and frozen-syst bands; they lie within the band except the last 1-2 bins of VR2/VR3 which are statistics-limited.
4. `design_2d_masym_deta`: QCD MC and data (A removed) in the plane with the region lines; the QCD populates all four regions (B and D dense) with no visible structure at the boundaries.
5. `design_boundary_scan`: kappa and proxy efficiencies against each boundary: kappa rises when the fail band widens (1.00 to 1.04) and VV/ttbar efficiency rises with looser masym/Δη, the chosen 0.2/1.2/0.4 is a compromise (kappa=1.008, VV eff 0.45).
6. `independence_ratio_vs_deta`: the other direction of the independence test, N(masym<0.2)/N(0.2-0.4) vs |Δη|: flat within ~1-3% in MC and data (data only for |Δη|>1.2 since the rest contains A; data ~3% below MC).
7. `substructure_dists`, `substructure_roc`: tau21, tau32, N2 of pooled jets, QCD vs W/Z-matched and top-matched proxies in a fixed mass window. They are real discriminators (QCD mistag 0.19/0.20 for tau21/N2 at 60% V efficiency; 0.14 for tau32 at 60% top efficiency) but I did not use them: they depend on jet mass (so a cut sculpts m_avg), the ranking `fits/rank_pairs.csv` showed larger kappa/m-dependence for t21avg pairs (e.g. masym x t21avg kappa 1.8), and I did not test them as a preselection cut; this may cost sensitivity.
8. `closure_map_mc`, `closure_map_data`: inclusive kappa-1 and pull over a grid of masym pass band (fail band = next 0.2) and |Δη| boundary. MC: +0.2 to +6.5%, the chosen point +0.8% (2.1σ) is among the best; the farther the masym band from 0 the larger kappa. Data only for pass bands starting at ≥0.2 (otherwise test-A overlaps SR A, shown blind): +0.7% to +4.1% with pulls 2-8σ.
9. `nonclosure_map`: obs/pred-1 and pull for the 7 frozen tests plus 11 MC variations per bin; black boxes mark the test setting the per-bin shape term: data VR1/VR3 at m_avg 120-300 and MC VR1/VR3 at low and mid m_avg, so the shape term comes mostly from the x-shifted regions, whose masym bands differ from A's.

Caveat from the new maps: inclusive kappa in shifted data regions is up to 4% (5-8σ) while the frozen correlated syst is 1.6%; these regions use inclusive kappa not the per-bin fitted TF used for the prediction (the matching data VRs give ratio 0.984-0.996), but the dependence of kappa on the masym band is a systematic effect of a few percent that the 1.6% term does not obviously cover. Observed/predicted in SR (0.99) is unaffected; nothing was changed.
